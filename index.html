About Me

Engineering reliable data, end to end

I'm a Data Engineer with approximately four years of industry experience across healthcare, oncology-related healthcare data, finance, insurance, marketing analytics, digital advertising, and real-time cryptocurrency data. My work goes beyond building new pipelines from scratch — a large part of my experience comes from working within existing enterprise architectures, where I contribute through technical decision-making, pipeline and orchestration improvements, data-quality validation, QA and release validation, production issue investigation, and authentication modernization.

I understand the full lifecycle of data — from ingestion and transformation through validation, deployment, monitoring, and downstream consumption — and I care about making that lifecycle traceable, observable, and trustworthy. Whether I'm reconciling pipeline outputs, profiling a new dataset, or investigating an anomaly against historical baselines, my focus is on making sure the data that reaches business users and analytics teams is accurate and dependable.
